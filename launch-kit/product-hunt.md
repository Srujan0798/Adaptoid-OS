# Product Hunt Launch Kit — Adaptoid OS v4.0

## Product Name
Adaptoid OS

## Tagline
The agentic operating system that prevents AI projects from silently failing.

## Description
Adaptoid OS v4.0 is an open-source harness for building reliable agentic AI systems. Instead of another framework, it gives you a sovereign core with 18 documented failure modes, 20 validators, 15 project archetypes, and one-command project scaffolding. Ship agentic software with guardrails, not guesswork.

## Maker Comment
I built Adaptoid OS after watching my own AI projects drift, stall, or ship broken artifacts. Most repos give you building blocks; this gives you the safety net. It merges the best patterns from LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen, and MetaGPT into an independent core — with optional bridges when you need their runtimes. v4.0 adds typed project intent, a memory bank, route sentinel, and OAP security.
