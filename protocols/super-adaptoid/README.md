The Super-Adaptoid layer is the v5.0 consciousness, memory-identity, and evolution harness that sits above the v4.0 Adaptoid Kernel. It is a protocol layer, not a product name claim.

## Relationship to the Kernel

Super-Adaptoid protocols load on trigger, just like other `protocols/`. They depend on the v4.0 kernel for principles, anti-hallucination rules, and validators, and they extend the kernel with self-monitoring and evolution capabilities.
