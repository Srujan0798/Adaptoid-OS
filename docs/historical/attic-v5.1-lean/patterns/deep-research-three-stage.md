# Pattern: Deep Research (Three-Stage)

## Context
Research projects produce shallow outputs because agents stop at the first acceptable answer.

## Pattern
Force three distinct stages with independent verification at each.

## Recipe
1. **Stage 1 — Breadth** — Collect 10+ sources, extract claims, no synthesis yet.
2. **Stage 2 — Depth** — For each claim, find supporting/contradicting evidence.
3. **Stage 3 — Synthesis** — Build argument map, identify gaps, write final report.

## Anti-patterns
- Synthesizing before collecting enough sources.
- Stopping at Stage 1 because "it looks good enough."
